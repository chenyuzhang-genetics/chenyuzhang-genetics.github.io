About

I study how tea plants take up, move and store mineral elements, and the genes behind those traits.

I am a Lecturer at the College of Forestry, Central South University of Forestry and Technology. I received my PhD in 2025, jointly trained by Huazhong Agricultural University and the Tea Research Institute of the Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences, with CSC-funded training in the Laboratory of Genetics at Wageningen University. My work combines plant physiology, elemental imaging, chemical speciation modelling and genomics, and extends from tea to related Camellia crops such as oil-camellia.
