About简介

Chenyu studies the genetic mechanisms that shape plant traits, and the evolutionary principles behind them.

张晨禹研究植物性状形成的遗传机制及其背后的进化原理。

Chenyu Zhang is a Lecturer at the College of Forestry, Central South University of Forestry and Technology. He received his PhD in 2025, jointly trained by Huazhong Agricultural University and the Tea Research Institute of the Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences, with CSC-funded training in the Laboratory of Genetics at Wageningen University. His work combines genomics, quantitative genetics, plant physiology and elemental imaging, and focuses on the economic crops of the genus Camellia.

张晨禹现任中南林业科技大学林学院讲师。2025年获博士学位，由华中农业大学与中国农业科学院茶叶研究所联合培养，其间受国家留学基金委资助在荷兰瓦赫宁根大学遗传学实验室联合培养。他的研究结合基因组学、数量遗传学、植物生理学和元素成像技术，聚焦山茶属经济作物。

Research研究

The question that drives my work我关注的科学问题

How did the economic crops of Camellia — tea, oil-camellia and ornamental camellias — come into being, and what genetic mechanisms underlie their distinctive traits?

山茶属的经济作物——茶、油茶和山茶——是如何形成的？它们的特色性状背后有着怎样的遗传机理？

SNP chips · population geneticsSNP 芯片 · 群体遗传

Diversity of tea germplasm茶树种质资源多样性

Genotyping natural tea resources from across China to describe their diversity and link candidate genes to traits.对全国各地的茶树自然资源进行基因分型，解析其遗传多样性，并将候选基因与性状关联。

Genomics · flowering基因组学 · 开花

Flowering in oil-camellia油茶开花

Genome resources, SNP chips and QTL analysis to understand flowering time and inflorescence traits in Camellia oil crops.利用基因组资源、SNP 芯片和 QTL 分析，解析山茶属油料作物的花期与花序性状。

QTL mapping · F1 populationQTL 定位 · F1 群体

Genetics of shoot quality traits茶树新梢品质性状遗传

Mapping loci that control mineral elements, catechins and alkaloids in young tea shoots.定位控制茶树新梢矿质元素、儿茶素和生物碱含量的遗传位点。

Ionomics · root physiology离子组学 · 根系生理

Fluoride and aluminium in tea roots茶树根系中的氟与铝
